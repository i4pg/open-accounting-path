# Tooling Tracks
> Concepts are necessary but not sufficient — modern accountants live in spreadsheets, cloud bookkeeping software, and increasingly in data tools. Work these tracks *alongside* the core curriculum, not after it. Learn the manual method first (so the tool is never a black box), then automate.

## Spreadsheets Mastery (Excel + Google Sheets)
*Why it matters:* Spreadsheets are the accountant's daily workbench: reconciliations, schedules, pivot-table analysis, lookups (XLOOKUP/INDEX-MATCH), conditional logic, and clean financial layouts. Fluency here directly drives speed and accuracy in close, audit prep, and ad-hoc analysis, and is the prerequisite skill for every other tool in this list.

- [Excel help & learning (official training videos and guides)](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel)  
  <sub>Microsoft Support · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Vendor-authoritative how-to articles plus the 'Excel video training' track (formulas, tables, PivotTables, charts). Always current with the latest Excel.
- [Excel tutorials (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, Formulas)](https://edu.gcfglobal.org/en/excel/)  
  <sub>GCFGlobal (Goodwill Community Foundation) · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free, structured, no-signup lessons. If link changes, search for: 'GCFGlobal Excel tutorial'. Same site also has free Google Sheets and Excel Formulas courses.
- [Google Sheets training and help (Workspace Learning Center)](https://support.google.com/docs/topic/9054603)  
  <sub>Google · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Official Sheets cheat sheets and tutorials. If link shifts, search for: 'Google Sheets Workspace Learning Center'. Essential because many SMB/B2B finance teams run on Sheets.
- [ExcelIsFun (Mike Girvin, Highline College)](https://www.youtube.com/user/ExcelIsFun)  
  <sub>YouTube (ExcelIsFun) · video-series · 🟢 Free · supplement</sub>  
  Thousands of free, accounting-relevant Excel lessons including full 'Excel Basics' and data-analysis playlists. Deep coverage of formulas and PivotTables.
- [Microsoft Learn — Excel training paths](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/m365/)  
  <sub>Microsoft Learn · course · 🟢 Free · practice</sub>  
  Free interactive learning paths. Browse all learning paths and search 'Excel'. Good for guided, modular skill-building.

## Bookkeeping Software (QuickBooks Online + Xero)
*Why it matters:* QuickBooks Online and Xero are the two dominant SMB/cloud ledgers most accountants and bookkeepers actually operate. Both vendors give accountants a genuinely free accountant edition plus free, certification-grade training, so this is the highest-ROI free credential an accountant can stack to be employable and client-ready.

- [QuickBooks ProAdvisor Academy — free certification & training](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/accountants/training-certification/certification/)  
  <sub>Intuit QuickBooks · mooc · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free with a free QuickBooks Online Accountant account. Includes QBO certification courses/exams and CPE-eligible webinars.
- [Intuit Bookkeeping (foundational bookkeeping certification)](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/proadvisor-academy/)  
  <sub>Intuit QuickBooks · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Now-free bookkeeping certification (formerly a paid Coursera course) requiring no prior QuickBooks knowledge. Good entry point for fundamentals. Search for: 'Intuit Bookkeeping certification ProAdvisor Academy'.
- [Xero advisor certification & Xero learning](https://central.xero.com/s/certifications)  
  <sub>Xero · mooc · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free, multi-level certification (L1 associate ~1-2 hrs). On-demand e-learning + assessment. Direct learning portal: learning.central.xero.com.
- [Xero Central — help & learning library](https://central.xero.com/)  
  <sub>Xero · reference · 🟢 Free · reference</sub>  
  Searchable task-level guides for every Xero workflow (bank rec, invoicing, payroll basics, reports). Useful day-to-day reference alongside the certification.
- [QuickBooks official YouTube tutorials](https://www.youtube.com/@QuickBooks)  
  <sub>YouTube (Intuit QuickBooks) · video-series · 🟢 Free · supplement</sub>  
  Short task-based walkthroughs of QBO features. Good for quick how-do-I answers without logging into a course.

## Data & Analytics (Power BI, SQL, Python/pandas)
*Why it matters:* Modern accounting is increasingly data-driven: pulling and cleaning data from ERPs, building dashboards instead of static reports, and automating repetitive reconciliations. Power BI handles visualization/reporting, SQL lets you query the source ledgers directly, and a little Python/pandas automates analysis at scale — the core toolkit of the analytical finance professional.

- [Power BI training (learning paths)](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/powerplatform/power-bi)  
  <sub>Microsoft Learn · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free official paths: 'Get started building with Power BI', 'Prepare data for analysis', 'Model data', and 'Prepare and visualize data' (DP-605 / PL-300 aligned).
- [Kaggle Learn — Python, Pandas, and Intro to SQL](https://www.kaggle.com/learn)  
  <sub>Kaggle (Google) · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free hands-in-browser micro-courses (1-7 hrs each), free completion certificates. Take 'Python' -> 'Pandas' -> 'Intro to SQL' in order.
- [SQLBolt — interactive SQL lessons](https://sqlbolt.com/)  
  <sub>SQLBolt · practice · 🟢 Free · practice</sub>  
  Zero-setup, in-browser SQL exercises that build SELECT/JOIN/aggregate fluency fast. Ideal first SQL exposure for non-programmers.
- [Mode SQL Tutorial (Basic + Intermediate analytics SQL)](https://mode.com/sql-tutorial/)  
  <sub>Mode · course · 🟢 Free · supplement</sub>  
  Free, business-analytics-oriented SQL tutorial with an embedded query editor. Strong on the kinds of aggregation/reporting queries finance uses.
- [Intro to SQL — querying and managing data](https://www.khanacademy.org/computing/computer-programming/sql)  
  <sub>Khan Academy · course · 🟢 Free · supplement</sub>  
  Free, beginner-friendly SQL course with interactive challenges. Good conceptual grounding in relational data.

## Financial Modeling Basics
*Why it matters:* Beyond recording history, accountants are increasingly asked to project it: budgets, forecasts, three-statement models, and DCF/valuation support for planning and deals. Learning to build clean, linked, assumption-driven models in Excel turns an accountant from a reporter of the past into a partner in forward-looking decisions.

- [Damodaran Online — valuation lectures, spreadsheets & data](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/)  
  <sub>Aswath Damodaran, NYU Stern · course · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free full valuation/corporate-finance webcasts (YouTube), downloadable DCF/WACC Excel models, and annually updated datasets. The gold-standard free valuation resource.
- [A Simple Model — free financial modeling course](https://www.asimplemodel.com/)  
  <sub>ASimpleModel.com · video-series · 🟢 Free · primary</sub>  
  Free, build-it-yourself video course on the three-statement model and DCF, with downloadable templates. Excellent for absolute beginners to modeling.
- [Financial modeling, DCF & three-statement explainer videos](https://www.youtube.com/@Edspira)  
  <sub>Edspira (Michael McLaughlin) — YouTube · video-series · 🟢 Free · supplement</sub>  
  Free, accountant-friendly explanations of DCF, NPV/IRR, WACC, and statement linkages. Search the channel for 'financial modeling' / 'DCF'.
- [Free finance & modeling foundation courses](https://corporatefinanceinstitute.com/collections/)  
  <sub>Corporate Finance Institute (CFI) · course · 🟡 Freemium · supplement</sub>  
  CFI offers several genuinely free courses (e.g., accounting/finance fundamentals, Excel) with free account; advanced FMVA content is paid. Search for: 'CFI free courses'.
- [Finance Theory I (15.401) — lecture notes & videos](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-401-finance-theory-i-fall-2008/)  
  <sub>MIT OpenCourseWare · course · 🟢 Free · reference</sub>  
  Free university-level grounding in time value of money, DCF, and valuation theory that underpins modeling. Rigorous reference, not a click-along Excel course.
